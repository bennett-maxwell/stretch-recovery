6% of Gross Revenues

Territory

3 miles or 15,000 people

Term

10 years + 10-year renewal

Renewal fee $9,500; then-current agreement may differ materially. (FDD Item 17, p.42)

Facility

400 to 1,500 sq ft

Standalone building or space in a shopping center or office building. (FDD Item 7, p.17)

Training

20 classroom + 30 OJT hours

Tuition-free; Ridgewood NJ, on-site, or virtual. (FDD Item 11, p.32)
